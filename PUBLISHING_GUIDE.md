# Publishing Guide｜上架指南

## 1. Repository
Use this repository as the public source of truth for EW AI Work Coach.

## 2. ChatGPT product identity
- Name: **EW AI Work Coach**
- Chinese name: **企業工作法教練**
- Developer: **Embodied Worker**
- Version: **0.1.0**
- Primary language: English
- Supported language: Traditional Chinese

## 3. Suggested ChatGPT description
**Turn workplace problems into structured actions.｜把工作問題轉成可執行的行動。**

EW AI Work Coach helps users choose and apply practical work methods, fill critical evidence gaps, and produce actionable work cards instead of management-theory essays.

## 4. Conversation starters
1. **Help me handle a workplace problem. / 幫我處理一個工作問題。**
2. **Help me choose the right work method. / 幫我選擇適合的工作方法。**
3. **Apply a method I specify. / 使用我指定的工作方法。**

## 5. Brand asset
Use the official Embodied Worker elephant trademark supplied by Embodied Worker Co., Ltd. Do not redraw, reinterpret, recolor, or regenerate the trademark.

Expected repository asset path:
`plugins/ew-ai-work-coach/assets/icon.png`

The same approved image may be used for composer icon and logo.

## 6. Pre-publish checks
- SKILL.md exists and matches plugin metadata.
- English and Traditional Chinese prompts are readable.
- No claim of automatic persistence, monitoring, scheduling, or external storage unless such capability is actually connected.
- No fabricated owners, deadlines, evidence, root causes, approvals, or results.
- Hypotheses remain labeled until verified.
- High-responsibility actions remain under human approval.
- Official trademark asset is used without regeneration.
